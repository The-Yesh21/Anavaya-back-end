# Constitutional Article Evaluation Matrix

## 1. Principles of Constitutional Triage
The Indian Constitution embodies substantive justice where personal liberty and vulnerable human rights supersede commercial or pecuniary claims.

## 2. Article Mapping Matrix
| Constitutional Article | Protected Interest | Trigger Condition | Mandatory Tier |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Article 21** | Protection of Life and Personal Liberty | Heinous crimes, bodily harm, child abduction, undertrial incarceration > statutory period | **High** |
| **Article 14** | Right to Equality before Law | Arbitrary state seizure, discrimination against marginalized parties | **High / Medium** |
| **Articles 23 & 24** | Prohibition of Traffic in Human Beings & Forced Labour | Child exploitation, forced servitude, human trafficking | **High** |
| **Article 19(1)(g)** | Freedom to Practice Profession or Business | Commercial injunctions, tax attachment halting enterprise operations | **Medium** |
| **Article 300A** | Right to Property | Deprivation of land/chattels without authority of law | **Medium / Low** |
| **Article 265** | Taxes Not to be Imposed Save by Authority of Law | Revenue recovery disputes, excise assessment challenges | **Medium / Low** |
