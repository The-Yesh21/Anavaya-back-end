# Statutory Evidentiary Compliance & Legal Notice

## 1. Electronic Records Admissibility
All trial audio recordings, speech transcripts, and algorithmic prioritization reports generated by Anavaya are structured in accordance with:
- **Section 63 of Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA), 2023** (formerly Section 65B of Indian Evidence Act, 1872).
- Cryptographic SHA-256 docket hashes for immutable evidentiary audit trails.

## 2. Judicial Discretion Safeguard
Anavaya provides assistive decision-support analytics only. In accordance with Indian jurisprudence, final judicial determinations remain the exclusive constitutional prerogative of the Presiding Bench.
